## Advanced

1. Naturally occurring copper consists of two isotopes: copper-63 (69.2% abundance) and copper-65 (30.8% abundance). Calculate the relative atomic mass of copper to 1 decimal place.
2. A magnesium atom (2, 8, 2) forms an ion by losing electrons. State the formula of the ion formed, including its charge, and give its electron configuration.
3. An atom of element X has 16 protons and forms an ion with a −2 charge. Determine the number of electrons in the ion and its electron configuration.
4. Explain why isotopes of the same element have identical chemical properties but can have very different nuclear stability (some being radioactive, others not).
