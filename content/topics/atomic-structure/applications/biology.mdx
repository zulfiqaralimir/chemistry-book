## Biology — Radioactive Isotopes as Tracers

Because isotopes of the same element behave identically in chemical reactions, biologists can substitute a radioactive isotope for its stable counterpart and *track* it through a living system without changing how it behaves chemically.

- **Carbon-14** dating uses the known decay rate of this carbon isotope to estimate the age of once-living material.
- **Iodine-131** is used medically to image and treat the thyroid gland, which naturally absorbs iodine to make hormones.

In both cases, it's the fact that isotopes share identical electron configurations — and therefore identical chemistry — that lets the radioactive form stand in for the stable one.
