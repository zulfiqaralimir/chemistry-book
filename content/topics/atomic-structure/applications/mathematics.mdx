## Mathematics — Weighted Averages

Calculating relative atomic mass from isotope abundances (Chapter 3) is a direct application of a **weighted average**:

$$
A_r = \frac{\sum (\text{isotope mass} \times \text{percentage abundance})}{100}
$$

This is the same mathematical idea used to calculate a weighted grade average from several assessments of different weightings, or an expected value in probability — a quantity where each possible outcome contributes in proportion to how often it occurs, rather than every outcome counting equally.
